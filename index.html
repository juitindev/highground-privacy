Highground Chess – Privacy Policy

Developer: Memory Mapped · Effective date: October 28, 2026 · 繁體中文版請往下

Summary

Highground Chess does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. The only data that ever leaves your device is an optional, anonymous record of finished matches, and only if you agree to share it.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (such as language and camera options), unlocked content, simple statistics (for example games played), and an unfinished game so you can continue later. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Optional anonymous match data

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous match data to help balance the rules and maps. You can change this choice at any time in Settings. If you say no, nothing is sent.

If you say yes, after each finished match the game sends:

  • the map theme and map seed, and the version numbers of the rules, terrain and app;
  • the game mode (two players on one device, or against the AI and its difficulty);
  • the list of moves, the result, the number of moves and the duration of the match.

This record does not include your name, email address, account, device ID, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it is not linked to you. We use it only to measure game balance and improve the game.

The data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Like most online services, Supabase may automatically log technical information such as your IP address when the game connects to it. We do not use this information to identify you, and it is not stored in the match records.

Purchases

If you buy the full unlock or other content, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties, and we do not use data for advertising. Aggregated, anonymous statistics about game balance may be published, for example in development updates.

Retention and deletion

Anonymous match records are kept for as long as they are useful for balancing the game. Because they contain no information that identifies you, we cannot find or delete the records of one particular player. You can stop sharing at any time by turning the option off in Settings.

Children

Highground Chess is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: memorymappeddev@gmail.com

高地棋 Highground Chess 隱私權政策

開發者：Memory Mapped · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

摘要

高地棋不收集個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告，也沒有任何廣告或分析 SDK。唯一會離開你裝置的資料，是你同意分享後才會傳送的匿名對局紀錄。

存在你裝置上的資料

遊戲會在裝置上儲存你的設定（例如語言、視角選項）、已解鎖的內容、簡單的統計（例如已玩局數），以及尚未下完的對局，方便你之後繼續。這些資料只存在你的裝置上，不會傳給我們。解除安裝 App 或清除 App 資料即會刪除。

選擇加入的匿名對局紀錄

第一次開啟遊戲時，會詢問你是否願意分享匿名對局資料，用來調整規則和地圖的平衡。你可以隨時在「設定」中變更。若選擇不分享，就不會傳送任何資料。

若選擇分享，每局結束後遊戲會傳送：

  • 地圖主題與地圖種子碼，以及規則、地形、App 的版本號；
  • 遊戲模式（同機雙人，或對戰 AI 及其難度）；
  • 棋步紀錄、對局結果、步數與對局時間。

這份紀錄不包含你的姓名、電子郵件、帳號、裝置 ID、廣告 ID、位置、聯絡人或任何其他個人資料，也不會與你連結。我們只用它來衡量遊戲平衡、改善遊戲。

資料存放在我們使用的資料庫服務 Supabase。和大多數線上服務一樣，遊戲連線時 Supabase 可能會自動記錄你的 IP 位址等技術資訊。我們不會用這些資訊識別你，對局紀錄中也不會儲存它。

購買

如果你購買完整版解鎖或其他內容，付款完全由 Google Play 處理。我們不會收到或儲存你的付款資料，該筆交易適用 Google 的隱私權政策。

分享

我們不會出售資料或分享給第三方，也不會將資料用於廣告。我們可能會公開彙整後的匿名平衡統計，例如在開發進度更新中。

保存與刪除

匿名對局紀錄會在對平衡調整仍有用的期間內保存。由於紀錄中沒有任何能識別你的資訊，我們無法找出或刪除特定玩家的紀錄。你可以隨時在「設定」中關閉分享，停止傳送。

兒童

高地棋適合 13 歲以上的玩家，並非針對兒童設計。我們不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新此頁面及上方的生效日期。
